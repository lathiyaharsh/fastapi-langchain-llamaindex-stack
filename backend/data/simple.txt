# Company Handbook

## About the Company

Acme Analytics is a software company that provides AI-powered business intelligence solutions. The company was founded in 2020 and its headquarters are located in Austin, Texas.

## Mission

Our mission is to help organizations make better decisions through trustworthy artificial intelligence.

## Products

### InsightAI
- AI-powered analytics platform
- Real-time dashboards
- Predictive forecasting
- Natural language querying

### DataSync
- ETL platform
- Supports PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, and BigQuery
- Incremental data synchronization
- Scheduled pipelines

### ReportGen
- Automated report generation
- PDF and Excel exports
- Email scheduling
- Custom templates

1. Employees must use multi-factor authentication.
2. Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
3. Laptops must be encrypted.
4. VPN is required for remote access.
5. Security awareness training is mandatory every year.
